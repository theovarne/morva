# MILONO Morphology

## Why a Mutable Body?

A rigid avatar can make identity appear tied to one silhouette. Amoebic morphology offers a different visual model: a soft body, mutable boundary, pseudopods, variable motility and internal flow can change while a recognizable core persists.

This is a way to explain **mutable form + persistent identity**, not biological evidence and not a cryptographic proof. A visibly stable nucleus cannot authenticate a key.

## Retained Amoebic Experiment

[amoeba.js](../dist/assets/amoeba.js) contains an alternative Canvas 2D renderer, not loaded by the active HTML:

1. **Membrane:** a deformed closed boundary with local pressure, seeded noise and bounded extensions.
2. **Cytoplasm:** sparse granules, compartments and flow traces.
3. **Nucleus:** a stable specimen-specific motif with bounded activity and displacement.

The boundary is fluid in appearance; no physical fluid solver or topological splitting/merging guarantee is implemented. Runtime time, viewport and input change the frame even though fixture parameters repeat.

The experiment models hover, contraction, drag and recoil. Its source is retained for research; its historical tests are not current-site coverage. See [Amoebic body model](amoebic-body.md) for detailed notes and [Interaction](../INTERACTION.md) for active versus experimental inputs.
